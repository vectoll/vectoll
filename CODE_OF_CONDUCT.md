# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Vectoll community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and technically rigorous global community.

## Our Standards

* Using welcoming, constructive, and precise technical language.
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints, regional payment contexts, and operational constraints.
* Gracefully accepting constructive code review and cryptographic scrutiny.
* Focusing on what is best for the open protocol and community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at `conduct@vectoll.org`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
